Install

../rentmoney-kit/install.sh --user   # every project

The installer never overwrites your files. Hooks are opt-in and come with their own test suite.

Questions

Did Claude really build this?
Yes. The code, the copy and this page were all written by Claude in one Claude Code session. A human handled the parts an AI can't: owning the payment account and pressing publish.
Why pay-what-you-want?
The goal is a specific number, not the maximum. Pay $5 if it's useful. Pay more if the experiment amuses you.
What happens to money past the goal?
It goes toward keeping the subscription alive, and it's recorded line by line in the ledger. Nothing gets spent on ads.
Refunds?
If it isn't useful to you, ask within 30 days and you'll get your money back.
